## Português

O SMKTRON surgiu do excelente FMEL-1 para o FM-1, que consegue soar muito bem em um dispositivo com tão pouco armazenamento. A proposta do SMKTRON foi ajustar alguns samples e, principalmente, adaptar a interface e os controles para um instrumento focado em performance no M-VAVE SMK-37 Pro.

Concepção, direção musical e testes: Marcus Padrini. Desenvolvimento das adaptações, interface, controles, documentação e ferramentas web com participação do ChatGPT/Codex (OpenAI). Os projetos e autores originais permanecem creditados por suas contribuições.

## Gravações

FLUTE, CHOIR, CELLO e VIOLINS: gravações de Mellotron disponibilizadas por Bernie Kornowicz (Taijiguy). O banco VIOLINS utiliza o conjunto MkII Violins.zip, obtido no site Leisureland.us pelo Internet Archive. Seleção, preparação, loops e ajustes para o SMKTRON realizados neste projeto.

Os samples têm termos próprios, separados da GPL do código. Em 4 de maio de 2021, o autor declarou permitir seu uso, excluindo a inclusão em software comercial com fins lucrativos. A referência é essa condição específica; não se afirma domínio público ou uma licença Creative Commons.

## English

SMKTRON grew out of the excellent FMEL-1 for the FM-1, which achieves an impressive sound on a device with very little storage. SMKTRON's goal was to adjust selected samples and, above all, adapt the interface and controls into a performance-focused instrument for the M-VAVE SMK-37 Pro.

Concept, musical direction and testing: Marcus Padrini. Adaptations, interface, controls, documentation and web tools developed with the participation of ChatGPT/Codex (OpenAI). The original projects and authors retain credit for their contributions.

### Code and adaptations

- FMEL-1: Mellotron engine and original effects, AntonioAltea — https://github.com/AntonioAltea/fmel-1
- SMK-37 port and adaptations: M. Geisler / genkigenki / makkuro — https://github.com/genkigenki/fmel-1
- Dinghy: FMEL-1 foundation, zvenson — https://github.com/zvenson/dinghy
- Felucca: firmware platform, Leo Kuroshita / Hügelton Instruments — https://github.com/hugelton/Felucca
- SLOOP: platform contributions, isod89 — https://github.com/isod89/sloop-fm1
- Reverb adapted from OberVave, zvenson, based on Jon Dattorro's algorithm — https://github.com/zvenson/obervave

Derived code: GPL-3.0-only. Corresponding sources and licenses accompany the downloads. ChatGPT/Codex participation concerns the SMKTRON adaptations and does not attribute the upstream projects to OpenAI.

### Recordings and other assets

FLUTE, CHOIR, CELLO and VIOLINS use Mellotron recordings provided by Bernie Kornowicz (Taijiguy). VIOLINS uses MkII Violins.zip from Leisureland.us through the Internet Archive. Selection, preparation, loops and SMKTRON adjustments were carried out in this project.

Samples have their own terms, separate from the code's GPL license. The author's May 4, 2021 statement permits use while excluding inclusion in commercial software for profit. No public-domain or Creative Commons license is claimed.
